How the numbers work
  • Achieved is premium booked in the selected period, up to the as-of date.
  • AOP is the committed target. Adj AOP is the internal stretch target set above AOP. Both are monthly, by RM and product. "Till date" counts a month's target in proportion to the calendar days covered; "Full month" counts the whole month.
  • Ach % AOP is Achieved ÷ AOP and Ach % Adj is Achieved ÷ Adj AOP. With full-month targets, the black tick on the bar shows how much of the period has passed.
  • Renewal rate is policies renewed ÷ policies due for renewal, for policies whose due date falls in the selected period. It comes from the renewal due list, so it rises as late renewals come in.
  • LY is premium for the same dates last year.
  • Show levels picks which levels make up the table. One level gives a flat list (for example every RM). Two or more give a tree in the usual order, Channel down to RM, with the levels in between rolled up.
  • Percentile ranks the rows of the lowest level shown. Top 95%ile is the best 5%, Top 90%ile the best 10%, Top 80%ile the best 20%, and Below 80%ile everyone else. Bottom 5%ile, 10%ile and 20%ile are the lowest 5%, 10% and 20%. The rows above add up only the rows shown.
  • Gr vs Prev compares with the period before: previous day (FTD), same days last week (WTD), same days last month (MTD), same days last quarter (QTD), previous month or quarter, or the equal span before a custom range. YTD has no previous period.
  • YTD Gr and MTD Gr always run from 1 April and from the 1st of the month to the as-of date, against the same dates last year.
  • Every row is the sum of the rows under it, starting from each RM.
